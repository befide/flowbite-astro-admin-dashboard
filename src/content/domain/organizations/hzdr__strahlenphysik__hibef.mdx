  en: The aim of the Helmholtz International Beamline for Extreme Fields (HIBEF)
    user consortium is to set up and operate various experiments at the High
    Energy Density (HED) beamline of the European XFEL (X-Ray Free-Electron
    Laser) with globally unique ultrashort and extremely bright X-ray flashes.
    It is organised as an international user consortium to ensure the highest
    scientific and technical level of the facilities provided, which in turn are
    available to all scientists. Research focuses on high-energy density
    physics, plasma physics, planetary geosciences, high-pressure physics and
    strong field physics, magnetism and correlated electron systems as well as
    material dynamics.

    ‘Universal’ in the name indicates that the facility can accelerate ions of
    all mass numbers from hydrogen to uranium. With the help of UNILAC, the
    elements Bohrium (Bh, 107), Hassium (Hs, 108), Meitnerium (Mt, 109),
    Darmstadtium (Ds, 110), Roentgenium (Rg, 111) and Copernicium (Cn, 112),
    among others, were synthesised for the first time.

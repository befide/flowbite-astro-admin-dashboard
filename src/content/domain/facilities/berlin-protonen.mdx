    Proton beams with an energy of 68 MeV are used to precisely target and
    destroy eye tumours while sparing surrounding healthy tissue. The treatment
    has a success rate of over 97% in destroying eye tumours. In most cases, the
    eye is not only saved, but a satisfactory level of vision is maintained.
    More than 3,700 patients have been treated at the facility since 1998.

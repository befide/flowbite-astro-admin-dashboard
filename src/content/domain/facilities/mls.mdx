  de: Speicherring für Elektronen mit Energien zwischen 105 Millionen und 630

  en: Storage ring for electrons with energies between 105 million and 630 million
    electronvolts and a circumference of 48 metres. The MLS stores beam
    intensities ranging from a single electron to tens of billions of electrons,
    with the intensity of the emitted radiation covering the corresponding
    range. Since the properties of the emitted radiation can be calculated from
    first principles, the MLS serves as a _standard candle_ for the calibration
    of other sources or spectrometers.

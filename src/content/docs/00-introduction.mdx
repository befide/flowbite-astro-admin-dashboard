## Abstract

The _BEFIDE Inventory_ provides extensive data on the accelerator research and development community in Germany. As essential part of the strategy process _Beschleunigeforschung 2025_, it aims at transforming decision-making from intuition or guesswork into a process grounded in factual evidence and analysis.

## The importance of data for strategy development

For the collaborative development of a strategy for a national research community, a data-based approach is crucial as it offers numerous benefits:

1. **Evidence-Based Decision Making**: Using data ensures that strategies are grounded in actual needs, behaviors, and trends within the research community, rather than assumptions or anecdotal evidence. This leads to more targeted and effective interventions.

2. **Reduced Risk and Bias**:: By relying on empirical evidence, a data-based approach minimizes the influence of personal biases and reduces the risk of costly strategic errors. This leads to more objective, reliable outcomes.

3. **Identifying Needs and Priorities**: Data analysis helps pinpoint the specific challenges, gaps, and opportunities across diverse subcommunities, enabling the community to set clear, shared objectives and prioritize resources where they are most needed.

4. **Facilitating Collaboration and Transparency**: A data-driven process provides a common factual basis for discussion, making it easier for stakeholders from different backgrounds to align on goals and actions. Approachable and transparent sharing of findings empower all participants and foster trust.

5. **Continuous Improvement**: By tracking metrics based on updated inventory data, the community can iteratively refine its strategy, learning what works and what doesn’t. This adaptability is essential for long-term success in a rapidly changing research landscape and

6. **Engaging Community Members**: Integrating data literacy and participatory analysis enables researchers and stakeholders to actively contribute to strategy development, ensuring that the approach is inclusive and responsive to the community’s evolving needs.

7. **Forecasting and Future-Proofing**: Historical data allows for allow the community to anticipate future trends and challenges, ensuring the strategy remains relevant and effective over time

A data-based approach transforms strategy development from a top-down, intuition-driven process into a collaborative, transparent, and adaptive effort—maximizing the impact and resilience of a national research community

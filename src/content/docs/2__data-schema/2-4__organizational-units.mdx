The **organizational units** schema describes all organizational units that are stakeholders with respect to the inventory.

Organizational units can be nested: each unit may have (at most) one parent.

### People Fields

To determine the number of people in the community, each unit has assigned a count of individuals, categorized by three dimensions:

- **Gender:** `male`, `female`, `nonbinary`
- **Discipline:** `physics`, `engineering`, `other`
- **Career Level:** `professor`, `seniorResearcher`, `postDoc`, `phdStudent`, `masterStudent`, `bachelorStudent`

The cartesian product of these three dimensions yields **54 distinct fields** (most will be zero for a given unit).

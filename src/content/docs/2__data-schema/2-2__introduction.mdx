## Data model

The inventory comprises entities that are connected by relations. The types of the entities and the relationships are backed by an ontology. In a simplified mannner, the ontology can be view as a taxonomy of classes in a tree-like strucuture, i.e. a class has at most one parent.

The inventory consists of entities (`Person`, `Course`, `OrganizationalUnit`, `Thesis`, `Facility`) connected by defined relationships.

The entity types and their relationships are governed by an ontology, i. e. the entities are instances of classes of that ontology.

The ontology comprises entities of type `Class` hat are organized in a tree-like hierarchy (each class has at most one parent, a child and its parent is connected by a isA relationship).

The ontology also defines richer relationships (object properties) between entities, enabling connections such as:

- Person `isHeadOf` OrganizationalUnit
- Thesis `hasAuthor` Person
- Facility `hasParts` Facility
- Facility `hasSuccessor` Facility
- Course `isOfferedBy` OrganizationalUnit

This approach allows for both hierarchical classification and complex inter-entity associations, supporting a flexible and semantically rich inventory model.
